Subject: Try CallKeeper, an accessible way to organize your meetings

Hi everyone,

I'd like to invite you to try CallKeeper, the new name for Meeting Directory. It's a Windows program designed around screen readers and keyboard navigation that gives you one place to save, find and open your Zoom and Teams meetings.

CallKeeper includes one-time and recurring schedules, time-zone conversion, favorites, categories, today's meetings, optional reminders and invitation importing. Choose a saved meeting and press Enter to open it in your usual meeting app or browser.

Download CallKeeper 0.4:
https://github.com/TarHeelBel/tarheelbel.GitHub.io/releases/download/v0.4.0/CallKeeper_0.4_Windows.zip

Extract the ZIP and open CallKeeper.exe. If you used Meeting Directory, back up and close it first; your saved meetings and settings load automatically on the same Windows account. No programming tools are needed.

This is an unsigned 64-bit Windows preview, so Windows may display a publisher notice. Reminders work while CallKeeper is open, including when minimized.

Please reply with what worked, anything confusing, and your screen reader and version. Please keep private meeting links, passcodes and backups out of feedback.

Thanks for helping me make this useful!
